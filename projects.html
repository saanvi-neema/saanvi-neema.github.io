Selective Verification for Multi-Agent AI Workflows

An online algorithm that decides which steps of a multi-agent AI workflow to verify, lowering checking costs while stopping errors from spreading downstream. I'm building a fault simulator, an offline-optimal solver (integer programming and dynamic programming), and a calibrated risk-based decision policy, benchmarked against four baselines and tested on real LangGraph agent pipelines.

TheraTrack

An iOS app that guides at-home physical therapy. It tracks the body in 3D with Apple's Vision framework, calculates joint angles to count reps and check range of motion, and gives real-time spoken corrections. An LLM extracts personalized limits from a therapist's notes or uploaded exercise sheets, and the app auto-detects exercise changes and generates progress reports.
